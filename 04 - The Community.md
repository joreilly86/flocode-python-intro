# **Welcome to the Flocode Community for Engineers**

## **A Unique Platform for Engineers**

The Flocode community is a dedicated space for engineers worldwide who are learning Python. While there are many Python communities for computer programmers, web developers, data science professionals and other industries, professional engineering is a unique niche that requires a different outlook due to the complexities of design code compliance, professional liability, and other profession-specific aspects. The Flocode community aims to fill this gap by providing a platform for engineers to progress on their Python learning journey.

Progression in Engineering is what it’s really all about. Personal progression is one of the most rewarding aspects of our profession. And somehow, we all do it in a vacuum. Some of us are lucky to have cool people we work with, engaged mentors or inspiring professors but those people are in the minority.

Technically, from an engineering perspective, the vision for flocode is simple, provide a platform where engineers can learn to code without being overwhelmed. But that’s just one facet of Flocode.

## **Creating a Positive Space for Connection**

The community will run alongside the Flocode course curriculum, serving as a place for students to discuss ongoing projects and collaborate with one another. Additionally, it will function as a growing repository of knowledge for those interested in contributing to the engineering coding community. This could include anything from discussions on preferred engineering software tools, workflows, and techniques to your personal Python packages that solve your engineering problems.

This community will provide engineers with a space to connect, a place for engineers by engineers. 

Learning together fosters a sense of camaraderie and makes it easier to progress and build momentum. The Flocode community is not about pontificating or dictating how things should be done; instead, it's a place where engineers can exhale, be themselves, make connections, learn and evolve. 

Our industry is corporatized, it's about delivering professional products in professional environments. There’s no escaping that to some extent, it’s what our clients pay for but this community will exist alongside that, while also providing a space for engineers to ask questions, seek help with their code, offer assistance to others, discuss career strategies, and even connect with like-minded individuals across the globe to pursue outrageous and fantastical entrepreneurial ventures. 

The Flocode community recognizes that everyone seeks enjoyment and passion in what they do. I love engineering but there are many things in our industry, culturally, that could be significantly improved.

Now for the first time, the tools exist to change this, and the Flocode community is at the forefront of this movement.

## The Principles

1. **Collaborative Learning:** This is simple. We believe in the power of learning together.
2. **Engineering-Centric Resources:** The community is designed to cater to the specific needs of engineers learning Python. Get answers to your coding questions, access curated resources, and learn Python with real-world engineering applications in mind.
3. **Mentorship and Support:** Benefit from the guidance of experienced engineers and, as you grow, pay that support forward to others. We foster a culture of mentorship and mutual support.
4. **Community-Driven Evolution:** Your voice matters. The Flocode community will continuously evolve based on your feedback and needs, ensuring that it remains relevant and valuable to you.
5. **Collaborative Problem-Solving**: The Flocode community connects you with fellow engineers to solve problems faster. Share your challenges, learn from others' experiences, and find innovative solutions together. 
6. **Respect and Inclusivity:** We will maintain a safe and inclusive environment by prohibiting discrimination, personal attacks, and the unauthorized sharing of others' work or data. Violations of this principle will result in removal from the community. I think this should go without saying but here I am saying it.
7. **Accountability Groups:** There will be optional enrollment to close-knit development and progression groups based on shared goals for focused support, motivation, and accountability.
8. **Community Fit:** To ensure a collaborative and respectful environment, we ask potential members to complete a short application. This helps maintain the integrity of the community and ensures that everyone is here for the right reasons.

## **Shaping Our Future Together**

---

The health and progression of any community depend entirely on its members. As the community grows, its direction and culture will be shaped by its members. People like you.

Let’s briefly talk about AI for a second, it’s the elephant in the room in almost every industry these days. While the advent of AI tools has led to a huge surge in self-coaching, self-learning, and self-direction…. community-driven initiatives offer an additional level of accelerated growth that can’t be matched by a large language model, not yet anyway!

I love these AI tools, I find them incredibly useful and fascinating and we’ll be digging into them in the flocode curriculum learning how to get the most out of them. 

At the end of the day, the accountability and interaction provided by a community can have a significant impact on an individual's resilience and ability to overcome obstacles encountered along the way.

It’s important to note that learning to code can be challenging, it often begins with a wave of initial enthusiasm because the topic is so deep and interesting and powerful, the initial learning progression is very fast, but after the novelty wears off, it can be tough to break through the inevitable plateaus that come with learning any new skill. 

Setting yourself up for success by leveraging both courses and the social aspect of a community is the best way to ensure real progress with coding. The impact of a positive social environment is profound in every pursuit, both professionally and personally.

For me personally, I’ve been training Brazilian Jiu Jitsu for a long time and the shared progression through a group setting is profound. Improved skill retention, accountability, connection and insights are all benefits of a community training and learning together. I know we’re talking about programming here but this is an aspect that unlocks major progression I would love to cultivate it within Flocode.

The community will be the heart of Flocode, and this is just the beginning of a much bigger long-term road-map for Flocode Community. That will be a topic for another day.
